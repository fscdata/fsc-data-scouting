Welcome to the FIRST South Carolina Scouting Alliance!

This is a site for collecting and analyzing data on FRC teams and matches.

Who are we?

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) teams in South Carolina have formed the Scouting Alliance to collect data on FRC teams and matches to help teams make informed decisions about their strategies and alliances. All data is freely available to any team that wants to use it.

How do I use this tool?

Use the "Scout" page to submit data on matches you watch. Use the "Reports" page to view and analyze the data collected.

I'm looking for more stats...

We are actively developing these tools and adding more information and statistics. For SC Charleston Qualifier, a summary page with supporting stats from other sources can be found here.

I have questions!

If you can't find an answer in the Info section of the site, reach out to the Scouting Alliance and we'll be happy to help.
