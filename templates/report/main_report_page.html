Match Data Reports

The Scouting Alliance data is available for any teams in the state to view. Depending on your scouting interests and preferences, you may find it useful to view the data in different ways.

The Export Page allows you to download all of the data for a given event in a CSV file. Teams who like to slice and dice information for their own rankings and calculations should start here.

The Scouting Alliance has compiled some common Statistics for teams based on what is available. These may be useful for comparing teams or identifying trends in performance.

There are also a few display pages of raw data. (Not recommended for mobile devices due to large tabular displays.)
